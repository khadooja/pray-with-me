## 1. Steps

### step: takbir

- Arabic name and level: تكبيرة الإحرام | (ركن)
- English name: takbirat al'iihram | The opening Allahu Akbar
- Instructions (English) and level: raise the hands to the shoulders or the ears | (سنة)
- Dhikr (Arabic) and level: الله أكبر | (ركن)
- Transliteration: Allāhu Akbar
- Meaning (English): Allah is the Greatest

### step: ruku

- Arabic name and level: الركوع | (ركن)
- English name: Rukū | bowing
- Instructions (English) and level: support oneself with the hands on the knees when bowing, fingers spread apart, arms away from one’s sides, back straight, not raising one’s head nor lowering it | (سنة)
- Dhikr (Arabic) and level: سبحان ربي العظيم | -مرة واحدة فقط- | (واجب)
- Transliteration: Subhana Rabbiyal- ‘Azeem | -once-
- Meaning (English): Glory be to my Lord, the Ever-Magnificent

### step: rising

- Arabic name and level: الرفع من الركوع | (ركن)
- English name: rising up after bowing

- Dhikr (Arabic) and level: سمع الله لمن حمده | (واجب)
- Transliteration: Sami‘allāhu li-man hamidah
- Meaning (English): Allah hears he who praises Him

### step: jalsa

- Arabic name and level: جلوس بين السجدتين | (ركن)
- English name: Juloos | sitting between the two prostrations
- Instructions (English) and level: Sit on your left foot, keep your right foot upright, and point the toes of your right foot toward the Qiblah. | (سنة)
- Dhikr (Arabic) and level: ربي اغفر لي | -مرة واحدة فقط- | (واجب)
- Transliteration: Rabbighfir li | -once-

### step: tashahhud

- Arabic name and level: التشهد | الأخير | (ركن)
- English name: Tashahhud | Reciting the tashahhud
- Instructions (English) and level: Sit on your left foot, keep your right foot upright, and point the toes of your right foot toward the Qiblah. and say: | (سنة)
- Dhikr (Arabic) and level: التحيات لله والصلوات والطيبات، السلام عليك أيها النبي ورحمة الله وبركاته، | السلام علينا وعلى عباد الله الصالحين، أشهد أن لا إله إلا الله وأشهد أن محمدًا عبده ورسوله، اللهم صل على محمد وعلى آل محمد كما صليت على إبراهيم وعلى آل إبراهيم إنك حميد مجيد. | (ركن)
- Transliteration: At-tahiyyātu lillāhi was-salawātu wattayyibātu, as-salāmu ‘alayka ayyuha an-nabyyu wa rahmatullāhi wa barakātuh | as-salāmu ‘alayna wa ‘ala ‘ibādillāh as-sālihīn; ashhadu an la ilāha illa Allah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan ‘abduhu wa rasūluh, Allāhumma salli ‘ala Muhammadin wa ‘ala āli Muhammad kama sallayta ‘ala Ibrāhīma wa ‘alā āli Ibrāhīm, innaka Hamīdun Majīd
- Meaning (English): All greetings, prayers, and good things are due to Allah. May the peace, blessings, and mercy of Allah be upon you, O Prophet. Peace be upon us and upon the righteous servants of Allah. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His Messenger,O Allah, bestow Your peace upon Muhammad and the family of Muhammad as You have bestowed Your peace upon Abraham and the family of Abraham; indeed, You are Praiseworthy and Glorious
- Audio: Google Drive file https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-z8PWEb_dMoQr3fxL-i2LSSJ0P3FsDwx/view?usp=drive_link -> save as public/audio/tashahhud.<ext>
- Sources: الاسم:[1] | التعليمات:[2] | النطق:[3] | المعنى:[3]

## 2. Al-Fatihah (ركن) — sources: meaning [4], transliteration [15] (license [16]), Amin meaning [19]

| Verse (Arabic) | Transliteration | Meaning (English) |
|---|---|---|
| بسم الله الرحمن الرحيم (سنة)[18] | Bismi Allahi arrahmani arraheem | In The Name of Allah, The All-Merciful, The Ever-Merciful. |
| الحمد لله رب العالمين | Alhamdu lillahi rabbi alAAalameen | Praise be to Allah, The Lord of the worlds. |
| الرحمن الرحيم | Arrahmani arraheem | The All-Merciful, The Ever-Merciful. |
| مالك يوم الدين | Maliki yawmi addeen | The Possessor of the Day of Doom. |
| إياك نعبد وإياك نستعين | Iyyaka naAAbudu wa-iyyaka nastaAAeen | You only do we worship, and You only do we beseech for help. |
| اهدنا الصراط المستقيم | Ihdina assirata almustaqeem | Guide us in the straight Path. |
| صراط الذين أنعمت عليهم غير المغضوب عليهم ولا الضالين | Sirata allatheena anAAamta AAalayhim ghayri almaghdoobi AAalayhim wala addalleen | The Path of the ones whom You have favored, other than that of the ones against whom You are angered, and not (that of) the erring. (It is customary to say "amin" "amen" at the end of this Surah). |
| آمين (سنة)[17] | Amen | [19] My lord, answer my prayer. |

Note: the basmala row and the Amin row are marked (سنة) by the content owner: teach them as Sunnah, never check them, and keep them OUT of the Fatihah "reference" text used for checking.
